An original worship song written by Jason Turner and produced by Yona Tree. When Adam disobeyed, the ground brought forth thorns and thistles — yet among the thorns there was a rose. The song follows that sign of hope to Calvary, where the Savior wore a crown of thorns and bled "a lover's red," and on to the Rose of Sharon raised by the Father's breath of power. Recorded tuned to A444. Full lyrics are available in the video description on YouTube.
