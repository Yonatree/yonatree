In the final installment of *Seven Lamps Before the Throne*, Yona Tree closes the series with a trilogy of parables from Luke 15 that reveal the loving heart of the Holy Trinity toward lost humanity.

- **The Lost Sheep** portrays God the Son as the Good Shepherd who searches for the lost.
- **The Lost Coin** portrays the Holy Spirit — in Yona Tree's interpretation — as the woman who lights a candle and searches diligently until what was lost is found.
- **The Prodigal Son** portrays God the Father, who sees His child a great way off and runs to embrace him.

A lost sheep, a lost coin, a lost child. A shepherd, a woman, a father. Salvation comes *from* the Father, *through* the Son, *by* the transforming work of the Holy Spirit — and it cannot be earned. The study ends with the vision of Revelation 22, where "the Spirit and the bride say, Come," and a final summary of the offices of each Member of the Holy Trinity.
