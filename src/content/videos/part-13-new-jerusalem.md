Part 13 concludes the study of the symbolic similarities between the Holy Spirit and the Church, then turns to one of the most fascinating symbols of the series: the Tree of Life.

Revelation 19 describes the Bride as having made herself ready, arrayed in fine linen — the righteousness of saints received from the Lamb. In Revelation 21, John sees the holy city, New Jerusalem, "prepared as a bride adorned for her husband." If the future city is compared to the Bride, what of the Jerusalem which is above *now*? Paul answers: "Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all" (Galatians 4:26). An elder Jerusalem and a younger Jerusalem — like Mother, like daughter.

The episode also considers the Spirit's intercession (Romans 8:26), then notes that the seven lamps before the throne find an earthly counterpart in the menorah, a lampstand resembling a burning almond tree — pointing to the burning bush and, before it, the Tree of Life.
