## Genesis — the tree in the midst of the garden

God planted a garden eastward in Eden, and in the midst of it "the tree of life also" (Genesis 2:9). After the fall, the way to the tree was closed: "lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever… he placed at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life" (Genesis 3:22–24).

Eden was a paradise where God walked with humanity — a picture of heaven. What was lost there is the story the rest of the Bible sets out to restore.

## Proverbs — Wisdom is a tree of life

"She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her" (Proverbs 3:18). Proverbs returns to the image: "The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life" (11:30); "when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life" (13:12); "a wholesome tongue is a tree of life" (15:4).

## The tabernacle — a tree of fire

The menorah was shaped like an almond tree, its seven lamps burning continually before the LORD. It recalls the burning bush where God called Moses — "the bush burned with fire, and the bush was not consumed" (Exodus 3:2). [Explore the Seven Lamps study](/studies/seven-lamps/).

## The prophets — trees beside the river

Ezekiel saw water flowing from under the threshold of the temple, deepening into a river. "And by the river upon the bank thereof, on this side and on that side, shall grow all trees for meat… and the fruit thereof shall be for meat, and the leaf thereof for medicine" (Ezekiel 47:12). The righteous person, too, is "like a tree planted by the rivers of water" (Psalm 1:3).

## The Gospel — rivers of living water

On the last day of the feast, Jesus cried, "If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink. He that believeth on me… out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water. (But this spake he of the Spirit…)" (John 7:37–39). And the Spirit bears fruit: "love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance" (Galatians 5:22–23).

## Revelation — the tree restored

> And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb. In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month: and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.
>
> — Revelation 22:1–2

"To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God" (Revelation 2:7). What the flaming sword once guarded is open to the redeemed.

:::yonatree
The oldest symbol for the Holy Spirit is the Tree of Life. She is guarded by a sword of fire — the Sword of the Spirit, the Word of God, Jesus Christ — so that no one comes to eternal life except through Him. The burning bush and the seven-branched menorah are variations on the same theme. The Church, made worthy to approach the Tree by the righteousness of her Bridegroom, takes on the nature of the Tree herself: the daughter becoming like her Spiritual Mother.
:::

The Tree of Life is finally about life with God: "And there shall be no more curse… and they shall see his face" (Revelation 22:3–4). [Read: The Flaming Sword and the Sword of the Spirit](/articles/the-sword-of-the-spirit/).
