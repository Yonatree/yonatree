> So he drove out the man; and he placed at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life.
>
> — Genesis 3:24

## The Sword of the Spirit

Scripture names only one sword that belongs to the Spirit: "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God" (Ephesians 6:17). "For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword" (Hebrews 4:12). And when John sees the risen Christ ride out of heaven, "his name is called The Word of God… and out of his mouth goeth a sharp sword" (Revelation 19:13, 15).

:::scripture
"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God… And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us" (John 1:1, 14). The Word of God became flesh when Jesus Christ was born into this world.
:::

At Pentecost, the Spirit came with "a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind" and "cloven tongues like as of fire" (Acts 2:2–3). Wind and fire; breath and word.

:::yonatree
The Tree of Life represents the plume of God's Breath — the Spirit. The flaming sword represents the fiery tongue of God's Word — the Son. Jesus is the fiery Sword of Truth standing in the way of anyone who would approach the Tree of Life. One must pass through the Sword of the Spirit (Christ, the living Word) to reach the Spirit Herself (the Tree of eternal life).
:::

## There is no other way

> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.
>
> — John 14:6

You cannot come to the Father without first being born of the Spirit, and you cannot be born of the Spirit without first acknowledging the Truth. The Truth is the Word of God, and the Word of God is His Son. "Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God" (1 John 4:2).

## Body, soul and spirit

Paul prays that believers' "whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ" (1 Thessalonians 5:23).

:::yonatree
As creatures made in the image of the Holy Trinity, we have three parts to our being: a soul, as the Trinity has a Soul (the Father); a spirit, as the Trinity has a Spirit (the Holy Spirit); and a body, as the Trinity has a Body (the Son, in whom "dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily," Colossians 2:9).
:::

## Life, and the alternative to life

Physical life is not enough; we need spiritual life. Without it the spirit is already dead — which is why it must be reborn. Scripture speaks soberly of what lies beyond this life:

> The hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation.
>
> — John 5:28–29

John saw "a great white throne," the books opened, and "another book… which is the book of life" (Revelation 20:11–12). To be written in that book, the spirit must be brought back to life; to be brought back to life, one must come to the Spiritual Tree of Life — through Christ.

## The word is near you

The Gospel moves from judgment to hope, and the way through the Sword is not hidden or difficult:

> The word is nigh thee, even in thy mouth, and in thy heart: that is, the word of faith, which we preach; That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved… For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.
>
> — Romans 10:8–13
