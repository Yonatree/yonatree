For centuries the Holy Spirit has been the least understood Person of the Trinity, often left ambiguous or improperly defined in Christian teaching. *Seven Lamps Before the Throne* sets out to answer one simple question — **who is the Holy Spirit?** — by examining how Scripture describes the Spirit's work in birth and rebirth, the symbols through which the Spirit is revealed, and the witness of early Christian writers.

The book explores biblical symbolism surrounding the Holy Spirit, Lady Wisdom, the sevenfold Spirit of God, the Tree of Life, the Bride of Christ, New Jerusalem, the Sword of the Spirit and the mystery of the Holy Trinity. It describes a Family of God that begins with the Trinity and extends to everyone begotten of the Heavenly Father, born of the Spirit, and betrothed to the Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Its central conclusion — that the Holy Spirit is revealed in Scripture with a feminine, motherly nature within the Family of God — is presented as Yona Tree's theological position. It is not shared by every Christian tradition, and the authors invite readers to examine the cited Scriptures and historical sources for themselves.
